How It Works

Three steps to your own VPN

No accounts. No sign-ups. Just add your server and tap Connect.

Add your VPS

Enter your server's host, username, and password — or import an Ed25519 private key. SSH2VPN tests the connection before saving.

Tap Connect

SSH2VPN raises a system VPN and routes all your iPhone traffic through an encrypted SSH tunnel to your server.

Browse freely

Your IP becomes your server's. Your traffic exits from your own machine. No third-party sees your data.

Privacy

Your data stays yours

SSH2VPN operates no servers and never sees your traffic. Period.

🔐

No logs. No servers. No intermediaries.

Credentials and keys stay on your device. The tunnel runs strictly between you and your server. We have zero access to your browsing data, connection history, or any personal information.
